A tiny retro ramen shop in Showa-era Japan. Customers keep coming to the six-seat counter.
Cook every bowl exactly as ordered and serve it fast. Your rating goes up, and the line
outside your shop grows longer and longer. How much can you earn before closing time?

■ 4 MENU ITEMS
• Ramen / Chashu Ramen / Negi Ramen / Negi Chashu Ramen
• Check the order slip on the counter and cook exactly what was ordered
• Wrong bowls and overcooked noodles go into the trash

■ A REAL COOKING FLOW
• Drop noodles into an empty hole in the boiler (up to 6 at once)
• Tap a bowl to pour the soup
• When a basket goes "Beep!", tap it to drain the noodles into the bowl
• Tap the chashu pork or green onions and they fly into the bowl
• Finished ramen goes to the customer automatically

■ DON'T FORGET THE WATER
• Ramen is hot, so customers won't eat without a glass of water
• Tap an empty glass to refill it

■ A BETTER RATING MEANS A LONGER LINE
• Fast, tasty bowls raise your rating
• More customers come, and the line stretches out past the window
• Keep them waiting too long and they leave angry

■ 3D RAMEN AND CUSTOMERS
• Steaming soup, wavy noodles, thick chashu and piles of green onions
• Lively customers with lots of expressions
• Retro Japanese pop-style music and sizzling sound effects
